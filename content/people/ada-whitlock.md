Ada has run the vertebrate collection since 2014 and is single-handedly
responsible for the fact that any of it is catalogued to a standard anyone else
can use.

Her work concentrates on the Wealden material, particularly the specimens
collected in the 1890s whose provenance records were lost and have had to be
reconstructed from correspondence.
